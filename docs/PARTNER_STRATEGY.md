# Pannas.com Partner Strategy: Global Fulfillment Hubs

**Date:** 2026-03-10  
**Project:** Pannas.com (Global Pan Delivery)  
**Objective:** Identify "Dark Kitchen" and Fulfillment partners in London, Dubai, and New York to support <60-minute local delivery via DoorDash/Deliveroo.

---

## 1. Hub Strategy Overview
To maintain the "Freshness Guarantee" of our Pan (especially the Meetha and Sada variants), we will transition from a "Cross-Border Direct" model to a **"Hub & Spoke" fulfillment model**:
1.  **Hub (Import):** Bulk air-freight of fresh betel leaves and premium ingredients (from India) to a central cold-storage facility in the target city.
2.  **Spoke (Dark Kitchen):** Small, localized assembly points where Pan is freshly prepared upon order.
3.  **Last Mile:** Integration with DoorDash/Deliveroo for <5-mile delivery.

---

## 2. London, UK (The "Karma" Hub)

### Recommended Partners:
*   **Karma Kitchen:** Provides flexible, high-spec kitchen spaces with cold storage. Ideal for assembling Meetha Pan. Locations in Wood Green, Hackney, and Bermondsey cover key diaspora hubs.
*   **London Grocery:** Potential logistics partner for warehousing and "Same Day" dispatch for non-DoorDash zones.
*   **Jacuna Kitchens:** Alternative dark kitchen provider with 70+ kitchens across London.

### Logistics Model:
*   **Inbound:** Weekly air cargo to Heathrow (LHR).
*   **Preparation:** On-site assembly at Karma Kitchen (Bermondsey).
*   **Delivery:** Integrated with **Deliveroo Editions** or **DoorDash Drive** for white-label fulfillment.

---

## 3. Dubai, UAE (The "Kitopi" Hub)

### Recommended Partners:
*   **Kitopi (Primary Partner):** The world's leading managed cloud kitchen. They handle the entire stack: storage, preparation, and delivery. Their "Hub and Satellite" model is perfect for covering Dubai's spread-out population.
*   **iKcon (by REEF):** Extensive network of cloud kitchens in the UAE. 
*   **Fresh on Table:** Can act as a sourcing partner for supplementary fresh ingredients (cherries, gulkand) to ensure local quality.

### Logistics Model:
*   **Inbound:** Daily/Bi-weekly air cargo to Dubai International (DXB).
*   **Preparation:** Managed by Kitopi’s trained staff using Pannas.com’s proprietary recipes.
*   **Delivery:** <30-minute delivery via Kitopi’s own fleet or Talabat/Careem.

---

## 4. New York, USA (The "United" Hub)

### Recommended Partners:
*   **Kitchen United:** Their Manhattan and Brooklyn sites are optimized for high-volume delivery. They provide the "MIX" platform which integrates all delivery apps.
*   **CloudKitchens:** Massive network across NYC (Queens, Bronx, Brooklyn). Best for "hyper-local" targeting of Indian neighborhoods in Jersey City and Floral Park.
*   **Baldor Specialty Foods:** Premium produce distributor. We can use their warehouse for cold-chain storage of our imported betel leaves.

### Logistics Model:
*   **Inbound:** Air cargo to JFK/Newark.
*   **Preparation:** Dedicated space in a CloudKitchens facility in Long Island City (central to NYC/Queens).
*   **Delivery:** **DoorDash Drive** API integration for <1-hour delivery in Manhattan, Queens, and parts of NJ.
