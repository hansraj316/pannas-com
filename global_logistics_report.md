## 2. Regional Compliance & Legal Requirements

### **USA (United States)**
*   **FDA:** Betel Nut products are under **Import Alert 99-39**. This means they are subject to "Detention Without Physical Examination" if labeled or used as food additives. Fresh Betel leaves (Piper betle) must be declared as fresh produce.
*   **USDA (APHIS):** Requires a **Phytosanitary Certificate** from India (NPPO). Must be free from soil and pests.
*   **Betel Nut (Areca nut):** Restrictive; processed (dried/roasted) is easier than raw.

### **United Kingdom**
*   **DEFRA/APHA:** Post-Brexit, imports from India require a Phytosanitary Certificate. 
*   **Salmonella Testing:** Historical bans (2014-2020) have been lifted, but 10% of shipments may still be sampled for Salmonella.
*   **Packaging:** Must be clearly labeled with the country of origin and botanical name.

### **UAE (GCC Region)**
*   **MOCCAE:** Requires an **Import Permit** and a Health Certificate. 
*   **Customs:** Generally business-friendly, but strict on "tobacco-containing" Pan (Gutkha), which is largely prohibited. Focus on "Fresh Pan" and "Meetha Pan" (Food grade).

## 3. Amazon Global Selling Requirements
To sell "Pan" on Amazon.com (USA) or other global marketplaces:

1.  **Category Ungating:** Requires approval in the **"Grocery & Gourmet Foods"** category.
2.  **Compliance Documentation:**
    *   FDA Food Facility Registration.
    *   Phytosanitary Certificate for each batch (for fresh produce).
    *   COA (Certificate of Analysis) showing the product is free from contaminants (Lead, Salmonella).
3.  **Fulfillment Model:** 
    *   **FBM (Fulfillment by Merchant):** Recommended for Fresh Pan to control shelf life.
    *   **FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon):** Only if the product is shelf-stable (e.g., Dried Pan, Pan Masala) with >90 days shelf life.
4.  **Labeling:** Must include Ingredients, Nutritional Facts (standard US format), and Manufacturer details.

## 4. Shipping & Logistics Recommendations
*   **Packaging:** Use vacuum-sealed pouches for fresh leaves with moisture-absorbent pads. Use insulated boxes with gel packs for Tier-1 long-haul (USA/Canada).
*   **Transit Time:** Aim for <72 hours door-to-door for Fresh Pan.
*   **Incoterms:** DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) is preferred for a seamless customer experience on Pannas.com.
